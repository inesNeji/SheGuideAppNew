The problem

Important information exists, but it is scattered and hard to follow.

After arriving in Germany, international women often need to manage visas, residence permits, Anmeldung, health insurance, banking, taxes, career decisions, family responsibilities and daily life at the same time. SheGuide turns this overwhelming process into a clear sequence of actions, adapted to the user's status, city and needs.

Scattered resources

Useful information is spread across websites, offices, videos and informal communities.

Language barriers

Many official steps are explained mainly in German and are difficult to decode quickly.

Mental load

Women often coordinate their own integration while supporting partners and family members.

The solution

A structured digital guidance app for arrival, settling in and integration.

SheGuide does not simply collect links. It guides users through personalized stages and connects them to trusted support when human help is needed.
